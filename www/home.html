One platform underneath the network

Power Connect is the customer experience. AgentOS is the operating layer underneath it — coordinating network, billing, identity, commerce and channels.

Network intelligence

Router and WiFi capabilities stay behind adapters, while the AgentOS core remains responsible for orchestration, policy, execution and observability.

Commerce without a second system

Products, carts, orders, vouchers and payments can use the same account, identity and channel layer instead of becoming a disconnected application.
