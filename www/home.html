Built for real work

From a field operator checking a site to a team completing a purchase, the same control-plane principles apply.

Observe network health

Poll devices deterministically, aggregate site state and surface actionable exceptions without putting a model in the critical path.

Resolve access and roaming

Keep tenant, site and session scope explicit while resolving connectivity and entitlement decisions.

Run commerce workflows

Coordinate catalog, pricing, inventory, checkout, billing and fulfillment through specialist ownership and clear handoffs.

Control changes

Turn proposed mutations into approved, idempotent actions with adapter evidence, rollback boundaries and audit records.

Manage projects as work

Break objectives into WBS packages, assign accountable specialists, track dependencies and close with evidence.

Operate across channels

Use web, mobile, desktop, CLI, Telegram, WhatsApp and APIs as presentation surfaces over the same governed runtime.

The specialist layer

Specialists own work; they do not become isolated mini-platforms. The roster is coordinated through common context, capability and policy boundaries.
