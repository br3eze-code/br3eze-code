About Power Connect

Simple on the surface. Serious underneath.

Power Connect is the customer-facing connectivity service. Its operating model is built around a domain-agnostic AgentOS control plane: specialists own work, policy governs authority, adapters reach real resources, and durable evidence records what happened.

The operating model

AgentOS is not one chatbot per domain. It resolves each execution across identity, scope, domain, specialist capability, resource, channel, workload and evidence. That lets the same operating model support connectivity today and broader commerce and operations tomorrow.

Principal→Tenant→Agent→Capability→Policy→Adapter→Evidence

Accountability stays explicit

Project Manager, Planner, Engineer, Designer, Accountant, Procurement, Expeditor, Secretary, QA, Security and Fleet Operations are accountability cells. The specialist roster adds focused domain teams without creating alternate control planes.

IdentityWho is acting and under which tenant.
ResourceWhich project, site, node, order or activity.
EvidenceWhat proves the operation completed.

Use cases

These are the kinds of work the architecture is designed to coordinate across the different domains and channels.

Fleet health and site operations

Poll nodes, aggregate site state, group alerts and expose read-only operational insight without model loops on the critical path.

Connectivity and roaming

Resolve explicit tenant/site/session scope, attach access and preserve auditable roaming context.

Commerce and POS

Move from catalog and pricing through checkout, billing, inventory and fulfillment with specialist handoffs.

Payment reconciliation

Authorize, settle, refund and reconcile financial state while preserving idempotency and evidence.

Project and WBS execution

Turn objectives into work packages, dependencies, milestones, approvals, handoffs and closeout records.

Cross-channel operations

Render the same governed work through PWA/web, mobile, desktop, CLI, REST/WebSocket, Telegram and WhatsApp surfaces.

Design principles

The system stays useful by keeping complexity behind clear boundaries.

Policy before power

Possessing a tool is not authority. Identity, scope, capability and approval are resolved before mutation.

Adapters at the edge

Infrastructure and providers remain replaceable integrations rather than leaking into the domain-neutral core.

Evidence proves work

Meaningful operations carry trace, actor, scope, outcome and evidence references for review and recovery.

Power Connect is the experience. AgentOS is the operating model.

The customer sees a simple service; the platform underneath is structured for controlled growth across domains.
