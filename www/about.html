About Power Connect

Simple for customers.
Structured underneath.

Power Connect is the customer-facing connectivity service. Underneath it sits a domain-agnostic AgentOS operating model designed to coordinate people, specialists, policies, tools and integrations without making customers carry the complexity.

The operating model

AgentOS is not a collection of unrelated chatbots. A request is resolved across identity, scope, capability, resource, channel, workload and evidence so the same governance model can work across connectivity, commerce and future services.

Principal→Tenant→Agent→Capability→Policy→Adapter→Evidence

Accountability stays explicit

Operational responsibility remains visible while specialist teams focus on defined work. This makes handoffs, approvals and outcomes easier to understand and audit.

IdentityWho is acting and under which tenant.
ResourceWhich site, node, order, project or activity.
EvidenceWhat proves the operation happened.

All domains share the same foundation

Domains add capabilities and adapters; they do not create alternate authorization or execution systems.

What this can coordinate

Representative use cases across the platform, from everyday customer access to governed operational work.

Fleet health and site operations

Observe nodes, aggregate site state, group alerts and surface exceptions without putting a model on the critical path.

Connectivity and roaming

Resolve explicit tenant, site and session scope while coordinating access and entitlements.

Commerce and POS

Coordinate catalog, pricing, checkout, billing, inventory and fulfillment through defined handoffs.

Payment reconciliation

Authorize, settle, refund and reconcile financial state while preserving idempotency and evidence.

Project and WBS execution

Break objectives into work packages, assign accountable specialists, track dependencies and close with evidence.

Cross-channel operations

Present the same governed work through web, mobile, desktop, CLI, messaging and APIs.

Three principles

The technology stays complex where it needs to be so the customer experience can stay clear.

Customer-first entry

The first step is always understandable: connect, choose, manage or get help.

Governed execution

Identity, scope, permissions, policy and approval stay in the control path.

Modular growth

Infrastructure and providers connect through boundaries instead of becoming the product itself.

Power Connect is the experience. AgentOS is the operating model.
