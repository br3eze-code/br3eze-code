About Power Connect

Connectivity first. Technology in the background.

Power Connect is built around a simple idea: internet access and connected services should be straightforward for the people using them. The technology behind the service is designed to support that experience without making the customer manage the complexity.

A service built to connect people

Power Connect brings access, account management and supporting services into one customer experience. The platform can work across supported connectivity locations while keeping the service focused on practical everyday use.

Built on a modular foundation

The underlying technology uses a modular architecture so network, commerce, payments and other integrations can evolve without forcing every part of the customer experience to change with them.

Separate the service from the infrastructure

Customer-facing functionality stays distinct from provider-specific infrastructure and integrations.

Use clear integration boundaries

External systems connect through defined adapters and contracts rather than becoming part of the core service.

Keep operations observable

Workflows are designed to be testable, traceable and easier to maintain as the service grows.
