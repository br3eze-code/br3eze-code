PRIVACY POLICY
Br3eze Africa · AgentOS Network Platform
LAST UPDATED: JUNE 2026
📡 INTRODUCTION § 01

Br3eze Africa (Pty) Ltd ("we", "us", "our") operates the AgentOS hotspot network management platform and associated services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information when you connect to our hotspot networks or use our AgentOS platform.

By connecting to a Br3eze Africa hotspot or using our services, you agree to the data practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please disconnect and refrain from using our services.

◈ This policy applies to all hotspot users, resellers, and administrators operating on the Br3eze Africa network infrastructure.
🗂️ DATA WE COLLECT § 02

We collect the following categories of information when you use our services:

DATA TYPE EXAMPLES BASIS
Connection identifiers MAC address, IP address, device hostname AUTO
Session data Login time, logout time, bytes uploaded/downloaded, session duration AUTO
Account credentials Voucher code, username, hashed password REQUIRED
Payment data Transaction reference, amount, payment method type (not card numbers) OPTIONAL
Contact details Phone number or email (if provided for receipt or support) OPTIONAL
Usage metadata Connected SSID, router node, location zone AUTO
⚠ We do not collect browsing history, web content, or decrypt encrypted (HTTPS) traffic. We only log connection-level metadata required for billing and network management.
⚙️ HOW WE USE YOUR DATA § 03

Your data is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Authenticating your session and enforcing voucher/time-based access limits
  • Calculating and recording data usage for billing accuracy
  • Detecting and preventing network abuse, fraud, and unauthorized access
  • Generating anonymised aggregate usage reports for network capacity planning
  • Responding to your support requests or disputes
  • Complying with applicable South African and regional telecommunications laws
  • Sending transaction receipts or service notifications (only if you provided contact details)

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

🗄️ DATA RETENTION § 04

We retain different categories of data for different periods:

  • Session logs — retained for 90 days for billing reconciliation, then automatically purged
  • Voucher records — retained for 12 months after expiry for dispute resolution
  • Payment references — retained for 5 years as required by financial regulations
  • Contact details — retained until you request deletion or for 24 months of inactivity
  • System audit logs — retained for 90 days for security incident investigation
◈ After retention periods expire, data is securely deleted from all active systems and backups within 30 days.
🔗 DATA SHARING § 05

We may share your data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers — cloud infrastructure (Firebase/Google Cloud) and payment processors, bound by data processing agreements
  • Resellers/operators — your connected reseller/hotspot operator may see your session usage data for their billing purposes
  • Legal obligations — law enforcement agencies when required by a valid court order or applicable law (RICA, POPIA)
  • Business transfer — in the event of a merger or acquisition, subject to the same privacy obligations

All third-party service providers are contractually required to protect your data to the same standard as set out in this policy.

🛡️ SECURITY MEASURES § 06

We implement industry-standard technical and organisational security measures including:

  • AES-256 encryption for data at rest in our databases
  • TLS 1.2+ for all data in transit between devices and our servers
  • bcrypt hashing for all stored passwords and voucher secrets
  • Role-based access control (RBAC) for all administrative functions
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Rate limiting and intrusion detection on all API endpoints
  • Firebase Security Rules enforcing data access boundaries
⚠ No network or internet transmission is 100% secure. While we take every reasonable precaution, we cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted over public networks.
⚖️ YOUR RIGHTS UNDER POPIA § 07

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA, Act 4 of 2013), you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to correction — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to deletion — request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Right to object — object to certain processing activities, including direct marketing
  • Right to complain — lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Information Officer using the details in Section 10. We will respond to all valid requests within 30 days.

🍪 COOKIES & LOCAL STORAGE § 08

Our captive portal and AgentOS web dashboard use browser local storage (not cookies) to store:

  • Your session authentication token (cleared on logout)
  • UI preferences such as selected view and theme settings
  • Cached router configuration for offline usability

We do not use third-party tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or analytics SDKs that transmit your data to external parties. Our platform uses Firebase Analytics only for aggregate, anonymised usage metrics with IP anonymisation enabled.

🧒 CHILDREN'S PRIVACY § 09

Our services are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will delete it within 5 business days.

Hotspot operators deploying our platform in schools or youth facilities are responsible for obtaining appropriate parental consent under applicable child protection legislation.
