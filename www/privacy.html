📡 INTRODUCTION § 01

Sandla Somusa Investments (Private) Limited ("we", "us", "our"), operating through the Br3eze Africa / Power Connect brands, is the proposed controller for personal information processed through these services, subject to confirmation of registered corporate details. This Privacy Policy explains how we may collect, use, store, disclose, and protect personal information when you use Wi-Fi services, vouchers, customer accounts, partner services, or AgentOS. The registered address and company registration number require confirmation and are not stated here.

This notice explains our processing practices; it does not make consent the legal basis for every activity. Where consent is required, we will request it clearly and separately where appropriate. You may choose not to provide optional information, but some service features may then be unavailable.

◈ This policy applies to all hotspot users, resellers, and administrators operating on the Br3eze Africa network infrastructure.
🗂️ DATA WE COLLECT § 02

We collect the following categories of information when you use our services:

DATA TYPE EXAMPLES BASIS
Connection identifiers MAC address, IP address, device hostname AUTO
Session data Login time, logout time, bytes uploaded/downloaded, session duration AUTO
Account credentials Voucher code, username, hashed password REQUIRED
Payment data Transaction reference, amount, payment method type (not card numbers) OPTIONAL
Contact details Phone number or email (if provided for receipt or support) OPTIONAL
Usage metadata Connected SSID, router node, location zone AUTO
⚠ We do not collect browsing history, web content, or decrypt encrypted (HTTPS) traffic. We only log connection-level metadata required for billing and network management.
⚙️ HOW WE USE YOUR DATA § 03

Your data is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Authenticating your session and enforcing voucher/time-based access limits
  • Calculating and recording data usage for billing accuracy
  • Detecting and preventing network abuse, fraud, and unauthorized access
  • Generating anonymised aggregate usage reports for network capacity planning
  • Responding to your support requests or disputes
  • Meeting applicable legal and regulatory obligations in Zimbabwe and any other jurisdiction that lawfully applies
  • Sending transaction receipts or service notifications (only if you provided contact details)

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

🗄️ DATA RETENTION § 04

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purpose, to resolve disputes, protect the service, and meet applicable legal obligations. Actual periods depend on record type, service configuration, legal requirements, and backup cycles. A documented retention schedule and deletion process must be maintained; we do not promise a fixed deletion deadline for every system or backup in this notice.

🔗 DATA SHARING § 05

We may disclose the minimum information reasonably necessary to service providers, network operators and partners, competent authorities where required or permitted by law, or parties involved in a business restructuring subject to legal safeguards. Providers and processing locations can change by deployment. Appropriate contractual and security safeguards for processors must be maintained as required by law.

Overseas processing: Information may be hosted or accessed in Zimbabwe and other countries through cloud or service providers. Before an overseas transfer or sharing, we will assess applicable Zimbabwean requirements, implement appropriate safeguards, and make any notification to POTRAZ required by law. Specific providers and countries depend on the service deployment.

⚖️ YOUR RIGHTS UNDER ZIMBABWEAN LAW § 07

Under Zimbabwe's Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07] and other applicable law, you may have rights to request access to personal information, seek correction of inaccurate information, object to or challenge certain processing, and exercise other rights provided by law. Available rights and lawful limits depend on the circumstances.

  • Right to access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to correction — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to deletion — request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Right to object — object to certain processing activities, including direct marketing
  • Complaint — raise a concern with us or contact POTRAZ as Zimbabwe's Data Protection Authority
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Information Officer using the details in Section 10. We will verify and handle requests within the period required by applicable law. We may need additional information to verify your identity or authority, and some requests may be limited by lawful retention or other legal obligations.

🍪 COOKIES & LOCAL STORAGE § 08

Our captive portal and AgentOS web dashboard use browser local storage (not cookies) to store:

  • Your session authentication token (cleared on logout)
  • UI preferences such as selected view and theme settings
  • Cached router configuration for offline usability

We may use browser storage, essential cookies, or diagnostic and analytics features depending on the application and deployment. Their exact use must be checked against the production build and provider settings. Where non-essential tracking or analytics requires consent under applicable law, it should not be enabled before required consent is obtained. Disabling browser storage may affect login or offline features.

🧒 CHILDREN'S PRIVACY § 09

Our services may be used in public, household, school, and community settings. Where personal information about a child under 18 is processed, we will apply the protections required by Zimbabwean law, including any applicable requirements for consent from a parent or legal guardian. A child's use of a hotspot is not blanket consent to unrelated processing. If you believe a child's information has been handled improperly, contact us using the details below.

Hotspot operators deploying our platform in schools or youth facilities are responsible for obtaining appropriate parental consent under applicable child protection legislation.

📬 CONTACT & DATA PROTECTION REQUESTS § 10

For privacy enquiries, data-subject requests, corrections, objections, or complaints, contact our privacy team. A formally appointed Data Protection Officer (DPO) contact will be published once the appointment is confirmed.
